

Averism treats the search for truth as a responsibility.

This does not mean that a person must spend every moment investigating everything. Nobody has enough time or knowledge to examine every question personally.

It means that when a person chooses to form an important belief, they should make a reasonable effort to understand why they believe it.

The effort required depends on the importance of the claim.

A person does not need to research every detail before deciding what food to eat. A claim that could affect someone's life, rights, safety, or understanding of reality deserves much more care.

The duty to seek truth also applies when the truth is inconvenient.

Averists should not deliberately avoid evidence because they are afraid of what it might show. They should not refuse to investigate a claim simply because they already know they would dislike the answer.

This does not require endless doubt.

Once the available evidence gives sufficient reason for a conclusion, a person can accept it and move on. Further investigation is useful when there is a meaningful reason to think that the conclusion may be wrong.

The duty also has limits.

A person is not responsible for knowing information that they had no reasonable way to obtain. Someone who reaches a conclusion honestly from the information available to them should not be judged as though they knowingly ignored information they had never encountered.

What matters is the effort they make once a reason to reconsider appears.

When strong new evidence challenges an important belief, ignoring it is a choice.

When a person discovers a serious weakness in their own reasoning, pretending it does not exist is a choice.

Averism asks its followers to take those moments seriously.

The search for truth is not finished when a person becomes an Averist. It continues for as long as there are questions worth asking and evidence worth examining.

There will always be things we do not know.

There will also always be opportunities to know more.

**Seek the truth when it matters, and do not turn away from it because you dislike where it leads.**
